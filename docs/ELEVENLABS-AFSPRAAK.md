# The ElevenLabs meeting, 28 October 2026

What the owner still needs from ElevenLabs, in the order it matters. Drawn
from `docs/ELEVENLABS-TERME.md` (§0, §4, §6, §12) and what the fal.ai
catalogue changed. The questions are in English so they can be read out as
they stand.

## Before the call (five minutes, no cost)

- **Is the ElevenLabs account in FUTUREBOXSTUDIO (Pty) Ltd's name?** The
  permission to let members use it rests on a "Business Entity", which
  excludes a person in a personal capacity (OEM Terms §1(D)). If it is in a
  personal name, ask on the call how to move it.
- **Is the training opt-out on?** Account → Terms and Privacy → Data use.
  It is not retroactive.

## 1. The licence: may members sell what they make?

1. "We are on the Business plan. We read OEM Terms §2(B)(f) as allowing us
   to make the services available to our members. Can you confirm that in
   writing, and does it need an Order Form, or is the plan enough?"
2. "OEM §1(I) defines an End User as using the service *only for its own
   internal business operations*. Our members are creators who release and
   sell their songs. How is that meant to read for them? The ElevenAgents
   terms say *or personal use* in the same clause."
3. "Are we a *Pure-play Music AI Creation Company* under the Music API
   Terms §4? If so, what exactly must the *powered by ElevenLabs* mark look
   like, and how does that sit with OEM §2(B)(d), which asks us not to make
   public statements without approval?"
4. "Please send the Music Terms, including the Prohibited Industries list,
   and your SOC 2 Type II report."

## 2. Keeping ElevenLabs as a backup

5. "fal.ai offers your models (music, Eleven v3 and v4 speech, dialogue,
   sound effects, voice changer, isolation, dubbing, forced alignment,
   speech to text). If we call them through fal.ai, does our OEM permission
   and commercial licence still apply, or only to calls on our own
   ElevenLabs account?"
   *Why it matters:* if yes, fal.ai is a second road to the same voices
   when one is down. If no, every ElevenLabs feature must stay on our own
   account, and fal.ai's copies are not for anything a member sells.
6. "What is the smallest plan that keeps the OEM permission? Can it be
   paused or reduced in a quiet month without losing it?" (Business is the
   lowest tier on the permitted side. Credits expire after 12 months, so
   never prepay more than will be used.)
7. "Do you have startup or volume pricing, or a programme for companies
   building on you?"
8. "What are our rate and concurrency limits on Business? We launch on
   1 November."

## 3. Voice cloning

9. "Do our members' voice clones count against our own slots (2,200
   custom, 10 professional on Business)? What happens when we reach them?"
10. "When a member deletes their cloned voice, is it gone at once on your
    side, and what is kept, for how long?" (Our deletion promise has to
    match.)
11. "For a professional clone, can your voice verification run inside our
    app for our member, so it is provably their own voice? Is anything like
    it required or available for instant clones?"
12. "Your Privacy Policy forbids under-18 voice data. We block voice
    features under 18. Is that enough on our side?"
13. "Is a zero-retention mode available to us on Business, so member audio
    is not stored after the job?"

## 4. Afrikaans

14. "Is Afrikaans supported in Eleven v3 or v4, for speech and for
    cloning? If not, is it on the roadmap, and when?"
    *Why it matters:* not one voice model in the whole fal.ai catalogue
    names Afrikaans. ElevenLabs may be the only road to a good Afrikaans
    voice, which is the strongest reason to keep it.

## 5. Smaller ones, if there is time

15. "Sound Effects has its own terms. Are effects made on Business ours to
    use commercially, and our members'?"
16. "Which of Music v2, Eleven v4 and the dialogue model are on our API
    today?"

## Afterwards

Paste their answers here or send them, and the app is changed to match:
the licence answer decides what the app may say about selling songs; the
fal.ai answer decides whether fal.ai becomes the backup road; the cloning
answers decide the deletion text and the consent step.
