# Drie kort briewe — Music.ai, Resend en Kling

Konsep vir Carli om te stuur. Een e-pos elk, dieselfde vier vrae, verskillende
aanhef. **Vul die twee plekhouers in waar jy dit stuur:** jou naam en rol
onderaan, en die kontak-e-pos.

## Hoekom hierdie drie kort is en die ander twee lank

`docs/EPOS-FAL.md` en `docs/EPOS-KITS.md` is lang briewe omdat daardie twee
verskaffers **video van 'n gesig** en **'n opname van 'n stem** kry. Albei is die
sensitiefste soort data wat deur hierdie app beweeg, en albei het 'n vierde vraag
bo-op die drie standaardes.

Hierdie drie kry minder: 'n e-posadres, 'n liriek, 'n prompt. POPIA artikel 72
vra steeds 'n grond vir **elkeen** wat data oor die grens kry — maar die
gesprek is korter omdat die risiko kleiner is, en 'n lang brief oor 'n klein saak
kry 'n stadiger antwoord as 'n kort een.

Stuur al drie op dieselfde dag. Dit is vyftien minute.

## Die gedeelde liggaam

> We're FutureBox Studio (FUTUREBOXSTUDIO (Pty) Ltd, South Africa, registration
> 2026/714071/07), a creative studio for music, voice and video. We're already
> integrated with you and approaching launch.
>
> South Africa's POPIA treats us as the responsible party and you as an operator
> processing on our instruction, and section 72 restricts sending personal
> information outside the country. Four short questions so we can state this
> accurately on our own privacy page:
>
> 1. **Do you have a standard DPA** you can send? A GDPR DPA is usually most of
>    what we need.
> 2. **Does it bind your sub-processors** to the same terms on onward transfer,
>    and is there a list of them and the regions involved?
> 3. **Do you train on customer data**, and if so is there an opt-out?
> 4. **How long is data retained** after a job completes, and what is the
>    deletion mechanism?
>
> Our privacy page names every company that sees a member's data and says what
> they may do with it — a check in our build fails if a supplier is missing from
> it — so I need to be able to write one accurate sentence rather than a hedge.
>
> Happy to sign whatever you have in standard form.

## Music.ai

**Subject:** Data processing agreement — FutureBox Studio (South Africa)

Open with: *"Hi Danny,"* if you are writing to the same contact as
`docs/EPOS-MUSICAI.md`, and add one line:

> This is separate from the capabilities and quotation note — purely the
> compliance side, which I'd like settled before launch rather than after.

**What they get:** audio you send for stem splitting and the jobs around it. Not
a trained voice, but it can be a recording of a person singing, so this one is
the sharpest of the three.

---

## Resend

**Subject:** DPA and data retention — FutureBox Studio (South Africa)

**What they get:** email addresses and the contents of the mail we send. No
audio, no video, no voice.

**The one extra question for them:**

> Do you retain message bodies after delivery, and for how long? Some of our
> mail carries a member's own words back to them.

---

## Kling

**Subject:** Data processing agreement and training policy — FutureBox Studio (South Africa)

**What they get:** prompts, and reference images where a member supplies one.

**The one extra question for them, and it is the one that matters here:**

> Where a customer supplies a reference image of a person, is that image used in
> training, and what is the retention term on it specifically?

A reference photograph of a face is the same category of data as the fal.ai
question in `docs/EPOS-FAL.md`, so if the answer to that one is unsatisfactory it
is unsatisfactory here too.

---

## Wat gebeur as hulle antwoord

Elke "ja, hier is ons DPA" is een reël op die privaatheidsbladsy en die
verskaffer is klaar. Elke "nee" is 'n besluit vir jou, en ek sal die alternatiewe
langs mekaar sit soos ek vir die video-enjins gedoen het in
`docs/ANDER-VERSKAFFERS.md`.

Nie een van hierdie drie blokkeer 'n funksie op die oomblik nie. Hulle is die
netheid wat 'n ouditeur gaan vra, en hulle is goedkoper om nou te vra as
agterna.
