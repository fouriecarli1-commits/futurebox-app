## Hoekom hierdie een eerste is

Dit is die skerpste item op die hele regslys, skerper as die fal.ai-een.

Kits.AI kry 'n opname van iemand se **stem**. 'n Stem is **spesiale persoonlike
inligting** onder POPIA — 'n hoër kategorie as 'n naam of 'n e-posadres. En ons
hou nie een van die drie dinge wat daardie oordrag nodig het nie:

| | ElevenLabs | Kits.AI |
|---|---|---|
| Kry 'n stemopname | ja | ja |
| Getekende DPA | **ja** | **nee** |
| Bewaartermyn gestel | ja, drie jaar | **nee** |
| POPIA 72-grond vir die oordrag | ja, SCC's | **nee** |

Daar is 'n **vierde** vraag wat net hierdie verskaffer het, en dit is nie
privaatheid nie — dit is lisensie. Kits se §1.1 verleen die regte aan **jou**,
die intekenaar. FutureBox hou die intekening; 'n lid nie. Niks wat ons gelees
het, spreek 'n platform aan wat die diens deurgee aan sy eie gebruikers nie: daar
is geen Kits OEM-terme, geen "Bundled Service", geen End User-definisie nie.

Dit is **nie 'n verbod nie** — §1.1 se toekenning is breed, en "make available"
is dieselfde werkwoord wat ElevenLabs se OEM-terme gebruik vir presies hierdie
ding. Dit is net aan ons toegeken, en nie aan 'n lid nie. Dit is 'n vraag om te
stel, nie 'n gat om toe te maak nie.

**Wat reeds gedoen is:** die bladsy sê hardop, by die naam, voor die knoppie,
watter enjin 'n ooreenkoms het en watter nie. Dit is 'n eerlike bekendmaking. Dit
is nie 'n oplossing nie.

**Subject:** Data processing agreement, retention, and end-user licensing — FutureBox Studio (South Africa)

Hi,

I'm the founder of FutureBox Studio (FUTUREBOXSTUDIO (Pty) Ltd, South Africa,
registration 2026/714071/07), a creative studio for music and voice aimed at
independent artists and small businesses. We're a Kits.AI subscriber and the
integration is built and running — voice training and voice conversion, both
through the API.

Four questions, and the first three are the ones our own compliance work has
flagged as outstanding against your service specifically.

## 1. A data processing agreement

South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) treats us as the
responsible party and Kits.AI as an operator processing on our instruction.

A voice recording is **special personal information** under POPIA — the Act
treats biometric data as a higher category than a name or an email address — so
this is the supplier relationship where a written agreement matters most to us.

Do you have a standard DPA you can send? A GDPR DPA is usually most of what we
need; POPIA's operator provisions (sections 20 and 21) cover much the same
ground, and we can tell you quickly whether it works as written.

## 2. Retention

How long do you hold uploaded training audio and converted output after a job
completes, and is that configurable?

If a member asks us to delete their voice data, what is the mechanism on your
side and how long does it take? We publish a retention term for every supplier
that receives a recording — ElevenLabs is three years on our page — and Kits is
currently the one we cannot state.

## 3. Cross-border transfer, and onward transfer

POPIA section 72 restricts sending personal information outside South Africa.
The ground we'd rely on is 72(1)(a): that the recipient is subject to a law,
binding corporate rules, or a binding agreement providing an adequate level of
protection, including provisions substantially similar to section 72 itself for
onward transfers.

So: does your DPA bind your sub-processors to the same terms on onward transfer,
and is there a list of sub-processors and the regions the audio sits in?

## 4. End users, which is a licensing question rather than a privacy one

Your Terms §1.1 grant the licence to **the subscriber**. We hold the
subscription; our members do not. Nothing we've found addresses a platform
passing the service through to its own users — no OEM terms, no bundled-service
clause, no end-user definition.

§1.1's grant is broad and "make available" is the same verb another vendor's OEM
terms use for exactly this arrangement, so I don't read it as a prohibition. But
it is granted to us, and I'd rather have it in writing than infer it.

Concretely: **when a member of our platform trains a voice model and converts
audio through our Kits subscription, who holds the licence to the output, and
are there minimum terms you want us to put in front of them?**

We already carry ElevenLabs' required end-user terms on our own terms page, so
if you have an equivalent we will publish it the same way.

## Wat gebeur as hulle antwoord

| Antwoord | Wat ek doen |
|---|---|
| DPA + bewaartermyn + sub-verwerkerlys | Privaatheidsbladsy kry Kits se volle reël, die waarskuwing voor die knoppie kom af |
| DPA maar geen bewaartermyn | Ons stel een by ooreenkoms, of die bladsy sê "onbepaald" — wat eerlik en lelik is |
| Geen DPA | Dan is dit jou besluit, en dit is 'n regte een: hou op om stemme daarheen te stuur, en die sangomskakeling hang daaraan. `docs/ANDER-VERSKAFFERS.md` hou die alternatiewe |
| §1.1 dek nie eindgebruikers nie | Ons terme kry 'n reël soos ElevenLabs s'n, of die funksie word 'n studio-funksie eerder as 'n lid-funksie |

Die derde ry is nie teoreties nie. Dit is die enigste item op die hele regslys
wat spesiale persoonlike inligting raak.
