# How to talk to the owner

Afrikaans, with the working words left in English.

She asked for this on 18 September 2026, and the reason is worth keeping:
the Afrikaans for a thing like a *sweep* is a word she has never had to use
and so does not read fluently, while the English one is the word she already
thinks in. Translating it is not politeness, it is an extra step between her
and what happened.

So: the sentences are Afrikaans; the names of the work stay English —
**sweep, probe, check, build, commit, push, merge, deploy, log, task**, and
the `check:*` names exactly as they are written in `package.json`. Do not
reach for *veeg*, *stoot*, *bou* or *saamsmelt* for these.

The same applies to anything with a real name: ElevenLabs, Supabase, Vercel,
Playwright, Turbopack. Names are not translated.

# Prices: her rules

- **80% profit on everything** (27 October 2026): an engine's cost is at
  most a fifth of what the cheapest credit sells for. Where she has set
  another figure for one press, hers stands (the living photo: 60%).
- **Nothing is free** (27 October 2026): *"Niks is gratis nie, alles wat 'n
  sinvolle funksie regoor die app is moet krediete kos."* A feature that
  runs on the phone and costs us nothing is still priced, in line with the
  paid products it sits beside, because that is where the profit is.
  Previews and samples that sell a paid press are not features.
- **Never the word "free"** in the app: "no credits" where something
  really costs none, and the price on every paid button.
- She sets the prices. Propose them with the engine's cost and the profit
  in rand beside each, and write her decision next to the number in
  `app/lib/credits.ts`.
